### A Compositional Theory of Curvature in Probabilistic Circuits

- Source: arXiv CS.AI
- Published: 2026-08-15
- Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.12869
- Matched layers: ai_compute

arXiv:2608.12869v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Probabilistic Circuits (PCs) are generative models that support exact inference and, unlike deep neural networks, admit an exact and tractable measure of loss-surface curvature: the trace of the Hessian of the log-likelihood. We show that treating sharpness as a global regularizer can be misspecified for PCs, whose curvature is inherently compositional.

### $\gamma$-weakly $\theta$-up-concavity: A Unified Framework for Non-Convex Optimization Beyond DR-Submodular and OSS Functions

- Source: arXiv CS.AI
- Published: 2026-11-13
- Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.13506
- Matched layers: data_center_energy

We introduce $\gamma$-weak $\theta$-up-concavity, a first-order condition for nonnegative monotone functions in which a scaling function $\theta$ lets the curvature change with the size of the solution. The resulting class strictly contains monotone DR-submodular functions and One-Sided Smooth (OSS) functions.

### A Hardware-Trained Generative Quantum Eigensolver for Sample-Based Quantum Diagonalization of Organotin Hydroxides up to 70 Qubits

- Source: arXiv CS.AI
- Published: 2026-11-13
- Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13755
- Matched layers: ai_compute, data_center_energy

We train generative quantum eigensolver (GQE) policies, transformers conditioned on molecular descriptors, to generate the state-preparation circuits for sample-based quantum diagonalization (SQD). These results demonstrate that circuit generators can be trained with quantum hardware at 70 qubits.
